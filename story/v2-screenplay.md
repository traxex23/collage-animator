# THE LONG STRING (v2) — wordless short, ~95s, 16:9

Logline: A widower's daily ritual with a patched old kite is broken when it crashes and tears.
Mending it at night, he unwinds a lifetime of memories, and finds something his wife hid inside
the kite on the day they made it, fifty years ago.

Rules (from The House of Small Cubes):
- No words. Music, wind, small sounds only.
- Present = cool grey-blue, near-monochrome. Memories = warm, glowing, saturated.
- Character is shown through behaviour and objects, never explained.
- Every object planted in Act 1 pays off in Act 3.

PLANTS -> PAYOFFS
- Two teacups on the shelf; he only ever takes one.  -> Final shot: he pours two.
- Ana's easel, covered with a cloth, untouched.       -> Her hidden painting is the reveal.
- The kite is covered in careful patches.             -> We learn who patched it and why.
- Knots on the string.                                -> Each knot is a memory.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACT 1 — THE RITUAL (0:00–0:28)
 1. EXT. HILLTOP COTTAGE, DAWN. Wide. Grey sea, wind in the grass. A single lit window.
 2. INT. COTTAGE. Tomas sits up slowly on the edge of the bed. Morning light.
 3. INT. KITCHEN. Shelf with TWO teacups. His hand hesitates, takes ONE. In the corner,
    an easel under a dust cloth.
 4. INT. HALL. The old red kite hangs on its hook. Close-up: it is covered in patches,
    some newspaper, some painted. He lifts it down like something precious.
 5. EXT. HILLTOP. He flies it. It climbs. For a moment he is almost young. (Knots on the string.)

INCITING ACCIDENT (0:28–0:36)
 6. The wind drops. The kite dives, strikes the rocks at the cliff edge.
 7. He kneels, holding it. The frame is snapped; the oldest layer is torn open.

ACT 2 — UNWINDING (0:36–1:10)   [each memory enters through a knot on the string]
 8. INT. WORKTABLE, NIGHT. Oil lamp. Glue, thread, scissors. He begins to unwind the string
    from the spool to re-tie it... a knot passes through his fingers —
 9. MEMORY: His granddaughter's first try. She crashes the kite, tears it, cries;
    he kneels and shows her how to patch it.  (who patched it: him, for them)
10. MEMORY: Ana by the window, thin, in a shawl, still painting a patch for the kite.
    Tomas holds her hand. Plum blossom outside. (the heaviest knot; music drops almost to silence)
11. MEMORY: Their daughter's wedding under the plum tree; the kite tied in the branches.
12. MEMORY: The daughter, age five, on Tomas's shoulders, Ana laughing, the kite high.
13. MEMORY (the deepest, warmest): The empty new house. Young Tomas and Ana gluing newspaper
    to bamboo. While he looks away, she paints something quickly INSIDE a fold of the paper,
    smiles to herself, and glues it shut.

ACT 3 — THE REVEAL (1:10–1:35)
14. INT. WORKTABLE, NIGHT. Back in grey. He peels back the torn oldest layer —
15. CLOSE-UP (the twist): Hidden inside, faded but intact, Ana's tiny watercolour:
    an OLD man and an OLD woman on this hill, flying this red kite together.
    She painted their old age on the first day. She always planned to grow old with him.
16. His hand over his mouth. A tear. Then — slowly — a smile.
17. EXT. HILLTOP, SUNSET. The mended kite flies, her painting now on the outside for everyone
    to see. Beside him on the bench, TWO teacups; he pours the second one.
18. The kite, small against a huge warm sky. Hold. Fade to black.
    Title: "The Long String"
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Music: one felt-piano theme. Sparse in Act 1, silent at the crash (only wind), rising warmth
through the memories, a near-silence at Ana (10), the full theme at the reveal (15-17), and a
single last note.
Sound: wind, kettle, a door latch, paper tearing, string creaking on the spool, lamp hiss.
