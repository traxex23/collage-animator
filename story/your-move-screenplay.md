# YOUR MOVE, a wordless animated short (~80s, 16:9)

**Logline:** Every afternoon a Filipino grandfather makes one chess move on the porch and knocks on his grandson's door. The boy
never looks up from his screen, until the day the knock doesn't come.

**Theme:** Time is the only gift you can't give back. And love keeps trying to find a way in.

**Rules:** No words, no on-screen text. Character is shown through behaviour and objects. One clear action per shot. Every planted
object pays off.

---

## Look & colour script (why this style)
Not the faded European watercolor of *The Long String*. This is a **warm Filipino home**, so the look is **hand-painted gouache with
soft coloured-pencil texture**: matte, humid, sun-warmed, like a Filipino children's picture book. The story is told with **two light sources**:

| World | Light | Palette |
|---|---|---|
| **Lolo's world** (porch, hallway) | late-afternoon sun through capiz windows | amber, narra brown, bougainvillea magenta, faded teal |
| **Migo's world** (bedroom) | cold screen glow in a dark room | cyan, violet, black; skin lit blue |
| **Absence** (after Lolo) | overcast dusk | the amber drained to grey-sepia; the magenta goes dull |
| **Memories** | soft golden haze, slightly overexposed | saturated mango-gold, warm pinks, glowing |
| **The move** (ending) | sunset | amber returns; the upstairs window is dark (screen off) |

The door is the border between warm and cold. Warm light is always trying to get into the blue room.

## Continuity bible (identical in every shot)
**Characters**
- **Lolo Ben**, 78: thin, warm brown skin, short white hair, thin white moustache, deep smile lines, thick black-rimmed reading glasses
  on a cord around his neck. Open short-sleeved **light-blue polo over a white sando**, brown slacks, **rubber tsinelas**.
  - `lolo_now`: upright, as above.
  - `lolo_frail`: same clothes, thinner, stooped (the cane and wheelchair are props in the shot).
  - `lolo_memory`: about 8-10 years younger, fuller face, salt-and-pepper hair, same glasses and polo.
- **Migo**, 13: slim, short messy black hair, **big black gaming headset**, oversized **charcoal-grey hoodie**, basketball shorts.
  - `migo_13` as above. `migo_8`: red-and-white striped shirt, gap-toothed grin. `migo_5`: yellow shirt, round cheeks.

**Places**
- **The house**: old two-storey Filipino house, wooden upper floor, **capiz-shell sliding windows**, galvanized roof, bougainvillea at
  the gate, a quiet barangay street.
- **The porch** (*balkonahe*): narra floorboards, **two rattan chairs** facing each other, a **low wooden side table with the
  chessboard** between them, a potted bougainvillea, capiz windows behind. The camera always sees it from the same side:
  **Lolo's chair left, Migo's chair right**.
- **The hallway**: narrow wooden corridor; **Migo's door** is dark wood with a few faded game stickers; warm light at the hallway
  end, cold blue light leaking from under the door.
- **Migo's room**: curtains shut, dark; desk against the wall, **one wide monitor glowing cyan and violet**, RGB keyboard, posters.
- **Lolo's room**: simple bed with a white cover, a small wooden altar shelf, capiz window, a wooden aparador.

**Props**
- **The chessboard**: old wooden board, worn pieces; **a white knight with a chipped ear** (Lolo's piece). Always mid-game.
- **Danish butter-cookie tin** (blue): the tin every Filipino home uses for sewing things. Here it's full of saved coins.
- **Lolo's phone**: small old Android, cracked corner.
- **The gift**: box wrapped in **newspaper, tied with red yarn**, containing the black gaming headset Migo wanted.

## Shot list
Seconds are on-screen time. Transitions are into the shot.

### ACT 1: The ritual
1. **(6s) Wide, the house, golden afternoon.** Quiet street; the porch glows amber; one upstairs capiz window glows cold blue.
   *Light: warm afternoon + blue window.* Camera slow push-in. *Motion:* bougainvillea sways, a tricycle's dust drifts.
   *Sound:* distant roosters, a tricycle, a radio playing faintly.
2. **(5s) Close, the porch chessboard.** Lolo's hand moves the chipped white knight, then turns the board slightly toward the empty
   right chair, inviting. *Motion:* his fingers set the piece down; he smiles toward the house. *Sound:* wooden click.
3. **(6s) Medium, the hallway.** Lolo (upright) knocks on Migo's door, holding up the white knight with a playful, hopeful smile.
   Warm light behind him, blue light under the door. *Sound:* two soft knocks.
4. **(5s) Over-the-shoulder, inside Migo's room.** Migo, headset on, lit cyan by the monitor, raises one hand without looking: *later*.
   In the ajar doorway behind, Lolo's warm silhouette slowly lowers the knight. *Sound:* muffled game sounds.
5. **(5s) Wide, the porch, rainy season.** Rain on the capiz windows. Lolo, now with a wooden **cane**, makes another move alone.
   Migo's chair is empty. *Motion:* rain, his slow hand. *Sound:* rain on the tin roof.
6. **(5s) Medium, the hallway.** Frail Lolo in a **wheelchair** at the closed door. He lifts the knight weakly and taps the door once.
   The door stays shut. Blue light under it. *Sound:* one faint tap.

### The accident
7. **(6s) Close, Migo's face.** Mid-game, he pauses and slides the headset off one ear. Silence. He looks toward the door:
   **no shadow, no light under it**. *Light: screen glow.* Camera slow push-in. *Sound:* the game cut to silence, then a clock ticking.
   (This is when he realizes the knocking stopped days ago.)
8. **(5s) Wide, the porch at overcast dusk.** Lolo's rattan chair is empty; his **reading glasses lie folded on the chessboard**;
   a white candle and white chrysanthemums on the capiz window ledge (a wake). *Light: absence, drained sepia.* *Sound:* only wind.

### ACT 2: Memories (reverse, warm golden haze)
9. **(5s) MEMORY, the same porch.** Migo (8) sits in Lolo's lap in the left chair; Lolo guides his small hand to move the white
   knight; both laughing. *Transition: bleed.*
10. **(5s) MEMORY, town plaza fiesta.** Migo (5) rides on Lolo's shoulders under colourful **banderitas**; Migo holds a cotton candy
    up to Lolo's mouth.
11. **(4s) MEMORY, the porch rattan chair.** Baby Migo asleep on Lolo's chest; Lolo dozing too, one hand on the baby's back.

### ACT 3: The reveal
12. **(6s) Medium, Lolo's room.** Migo stands in the doorway. On the white bed: a **gift wrapped in newspaper with red yarn**, and
    beside it the **open blue butter-cookie tin, full of coins**. *Light: soft window light.* *Transition: bleed.*
13. **(6s) Extreme close-up, Lolo's cracked phone in Migo's hands.** The screen shows **Migo's game lobby** with one tiny **level-1
    avatar wearing a straw salakot hat**, standing alone, waiting. (Lolo installed the game and was trying to reach him in his world.)
    *Sound:* the game's lobby music, small and tinny.
14. **(4s) Close, Migo's face.** Tears; he presses the phone to his chest. *Light: warm window light on one side, the phone's blue on
    the other.*

### The move
15. **(7s) Medium, the porch at sunset.** Migo sits in **his chair (right)**, the unopened gift beside him. He reaches out and makes his
    move: a black piece, set down gently. Lolo's empty chair across, the glasses on its seat. Bougainvillea petals drift.
    *Light: sunset amber returns.* *Transition: bleed.* *Sound:* one wooden click.
16. **(6s) Wide, the house at sunset.** The porch glows; **the upstairs window is dark** (screen off). Two small figures would have
    sat there; now one does. Camera slow pull-back. *Sound:* evening birds, the music resolves.

End card: **YOUR MOVE**.

## Music (score shape)
Solo **kulintang-tinged felt piano** (a soft gong/metal-bar texture over piano, subtly Filipino) with gentle strings.
- 0-26s: a playful little motif (the knock), repeated and getting sparser each time.
- 26-37s: the motif breaks off; silence and a clock.
- 37-51s: memories, warm and full, the motif as a lullaby.
- 51-70s: the reveal, the motif returns slowly, fragile.
- 70-86s: the move, resolves on one sustained note.

## Plants → payoffs
| Plant | Payoff |
|---|---|
| Lolo holds up the white knight at the door (3, 6) | Migo finally makes *his* move (15) |
| The empty right chair (2, 5) | Migo sits in it (15) |
| Blue light under the door (3, 6); the knocking fades (3 → 6) | No light, no knock; silence (7) |
| Migo's headset (4) | The gift is the headset he wanted (12); he leaves it unopened at the board (15) |
| Blue light vs warm light all film | The upstairs window goes dark at sunset (16) |
| The butter-cookie tin, a Filipino home cliché | Full of coins saved one by one (12) |
